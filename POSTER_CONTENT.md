# OPPORTUNITYOS

Students miss internships, hackathons, scholarships, and certifications because opportunities are scattered across multiple platforms.

**Solution:**
One intelligent platform that brings opportunities together and matches students based on their skills, interests, and goals.

✓ Search & Filters

✓ Student Dashboard
