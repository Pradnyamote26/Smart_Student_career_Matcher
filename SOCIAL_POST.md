🚀 Excited to present my project for the GDG FIT Pune Hackathon / FIT FEST 2026: **OpportunityOS**!

Students frequently miss out on amazing internships, hackathons, and scholarships simply because the information is scattered across the internet. 

💡 **The Solution:** OpportunityOS is an intelligent platform where students create a single profile and discover opportunities matched specifically to their skills, interests, and career goals. 

Instead of endless searching, the platform calculates a personalized "Match Score" and explains *exactly* why an opportunity is right for you! 🎯
